Mission briefing

Read the story, then complete three quick missions.

A group of students wanted to find out how much waste their school produced each day. They decided to set up recycling boxes for paper and plastic. They also asked classmates to sort out their waste carefully. At first, many students did not pay attention to the recycling signs, so the team came up with a simple points game. With more students joining the project, the team was able to carry out the plan for an entire month.

Six expressions to know

1. find outlearn or discover information · 알아내다
2. set upstart or arrange something · 설치하다, 마련하다
3. sort outseparate things into groups · 분류하다
4. pay attention tolook or listen carefully at · 주의를 기울이다
5. come up withthink of an idea or plan · 생각해 내다
6. carry outdo or complete a plan · 실행하다
Tip: Focus on the meaning of the whole expression, not each word separately.

MISSION 2 · USE IT IN CONTEXT

Complete the recycling plan

Choose an expression for each sentence. Each expression is used once.

1. We want to ______ how much plastic our class throws away.
2. The students will ______ three recycling boxes near the door.
3. Please ______ paper, plastic, and food waste.
4. Everyone should ______ the recycling instructions.
5. Our group needs to ______ a fun way to encourage students.
6. We will ______ our plan for four weeks.
